Why RGB555?

RGB555 packs 5 bits each for red, green, and blue into 15 bits. The Nintendo DS uses this color format, giving 32 × 32 × 32 — exactly 32,768 possible colors.

This picker selects every RGB555 color directly. The 8-bit preview expands each 5-bit channel across the full 0–255 range for display.
